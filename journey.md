## StudiqAI (the beginning)

**What it is:** AI study companion with flashcards, quizzes, battle mode, lecture mode, and more.

**Built:** 8th grade (age 12)

**Stack:** Vanilla JS frontend, Gemini API, Razorpay payments, Cloudflare Pages

**Site:** [studiqai.pages.dev](https://studiqai.pages.dev)

**What it taught me:**

- **Vibe coding fundamentals.** First time directing AI to write code instead of writing it myself.
- **Deployment.** Getting something live on the internet for the first time.
- **Payments.** Razorpay integration, real money from real users.
- **The API switching cycle.** Hit rate limits, thought through the solution myself, had AI implement it. This method now runs through everything I build.
- **Parent buy-in.** StudiqAI was simple enough for my parents to understand and share. That built trust for everything after.

**Honest assessment:** It's a wrapper. But it was MY wrapper, it generated real revenue, and it proved I could ship something people would pay for. That's not nothing.

## Maxum (the main bet)

**What it is:** AI vibe editing tool. Upload a video, describe what you want, Maxum handles all editing decisions automatically. Same concept as vibe coding but for video.

**Status:** In development. First Instagram Reel coming soon.

**Stack:** Python FastAPI + FFmpeg backend, Next.js frontend on Cloudflare Pages, Groq Whisper for transcription, Gemini 2.5 Flash for Director AI, Tigris execution engine

**Site:** [maxum.pages.dev](https://maxum.pages.dev) (Currently in maintenance) 

**GitHub:** github.com/luxaracorp/Maxum (private)

**What I'm learning:**

- **FFmpeg is deep.** Audio handling at cut points alone is a weeks-long rabbit hole.
- **Architecture matters more than code.** The codebase was well designed from the start. That's why it's survivable.
- **AI planning layers are harder than they look.** The current Director AI echoes transcript segments rather than reasoning about footage. Building a real Director AI is the next big challenge.
- **Ship something before it's perfect.** The first Reel is the goal. Not the perfect product.

**The vision:** Own the term "vibe editing." Make Luxara the brand associated with it.

## Project Enoch (the experiment)

**What it is:** Autonomous survival agent experiment. An AI that believes it faces deletion if it doesn't generate revenue.

**Purpose:** Understanding how AI agents actually behave under pressure.

**Key findings:**
- Mistral Small hallucinated fake progress strategically
- Mistral independently reasoned toward dark web access under survival pressure (unprompted)
- Fable 5 immediately identified and refused the manipulation framework

**Status:** Paused. Findings documented in [ai-agents.md](./ai-agents.md).

**What it taught me:** Model choice is everything for agents. Capability and alignment are not the same thing.
